Phase 4 — Agent Layer
Agent 1
Trend Monitoring
BiLSTM + z-score anomaly detection. Computes EMS (Emotional Momentum Score 0–100) from intensity × recurrence × acceleration. Maintains 7-day and 14-day rolling averages.

Agent 2
Burnout Risk
Computes burnout_risk_score 0–100 from EMS (×0.5) + z-score (×0.3) + positive emotion variance (×0.2). Score ≥ 70 triggers alert flag.

🔍 Longitudinal Trigger Detection — Scenarios 2 & 4 (★ Novel Contribution 6)
When mood is exhausted, the system identifies what caused it by cross-referencing today's journal topics with historical emotion+topic patterns from the past 7 and 30 days. Recurring co-occurrences of a topic and high stress are flagged as triggers.
Step 1
Extract today's topics
TF-IDF + spaCy NER on current journal entry → topic tags (e.g. "deadlines", "sleep", "argument")
Step 2
Cross-reference history
Query last 7 days + 30 days of (topic_tag, emotion_score) pairs. Count co-occurrences of topic + high stress (EMS >50)
Step 3
Flag recurring triggers
Topics appearing ≥3 times with high stress in past 7 days flagged as acute triggers. ≥5 times in 30 days flagged as chronic triggers
